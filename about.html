About SUDVEE

Authentic Indian pantry staples, crafted slowly from heritage farms to your table.

Purity from regional terroirs

SUDVEE bridges ancient Indian spice traditions with modern culinary simplicity. From sun-warmed Malabar coastlines to the volcanic soils of the Deccan plateau, we work directly with cultivators who have farmed the same land for generations.

Our masalas are stone-ground and cold-tempered, our sauces slow-simmered, and our noodles sun-dried from millet and heritage wheat — never with palm oil, preservatives, or refined flour.
